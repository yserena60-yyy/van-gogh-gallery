A GUIDE TO YOUR VISIT

There is more than one way in.

  1. 01

    Let the tour lead you

    Choose Highlights for a 3-minute visit through the halls. Full collection visits every verified work. Use Pause at any time; click a painting to stop and look closer.

  2. 02

    Look closer at a work

    Click a painting to enlarge it and read its story. Previous and Next inside the viewer move between works. Close it to return to the same place.

  3. 03

    Follow the person behind the paintings

    The story buttons stay visible on the right. They are optional reading paths, not steps you have to complete in order.
